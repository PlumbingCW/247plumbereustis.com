Emergency Toilet Repair in Eustis, FL

An overflowing or leaking toilet needs attention now. We repair running toilets, fix leaks at the base and install new toilets, and the price is clear before work starts.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

A toilet that overflows, leaks across the floor or will not stop running is a problem you notice immediately. For an overflow, lift the tank lid and push the flapper down or close the supply valve behind the toilet, then call our 24/7 line. Most toilet repairs can be done the same day.

Many toilet problems are small parts, a fill valve, a flapper or a supply line, and cost little to fix. Others, such as a rocking toilet or a leak from the base, point to the wax ring or the flange below. We find the actual cause, and if a new toilet is the better value, we explain installation too.

An overflow is a short window of choices. These steps limit the mess.

2

A toilet that runs, hisses or refills

The common causes are inexpensive and easy to find:

  • ✓Worn flapper that lets water leak from the tank to the bowl.
  • ✓Fill valve that does not shut off at the right level.
  • ✓Float height set too high so water drains into the overflow tube.
  • ✓Chain length that holds the flapper partly open.
  • ✓Silent leak that can be tested with a few drops of food coloring in the tank.

3

Leaking at the base or rocking

Water around the base of a toilet usually means a failed wax ring, a loose bolt or a cracked flange. A toilet that rocks has a flange problem, and a rocking toilet that leaks can damage the subfloor over time. Pull-and-reset is the repair, and a damaged flange may need a repair ring or replacement.

Water that appears at the supply line is often a worn connector, and replacing it with a braided line is a cheap fix. If you see staining around the base, soft flooring or a musty smell, call us before the subfloor is affected.

Installing a new toilet

  1. Choose the fit. Rough-in distance, bowl shape and height decide which models fit your bathroom.
  2. Price first. You get a clear price covering the toilet, supply line, wax ring and haul-away.
  3. Remove and inspect. We pull the old toilet and check the flange and subfloor.
  4. Set and seal. The new toilet is set on a new wax ring, bolted and leveled.
  5. Test and finish. We connect the supply, test flushes and check for leaks before we leave.

Toilets and Florida homes

Many Florida bathrooms sit on a concrete slab, which means a toilet leak can seep under flooring and show up as warped trim or a musty smell. Check around the base after any heavy rain or hurricane-season outage, because pressure changes can show a weak seal. If the problem is a leak in a supply line behind the wall, our leak detection service finds it.

8

Toilet rules in plain terms

Federal law limits new toilets to 1.6 gallons per flush, and many models use less. The IPC also sets how a toilet must be set, sealed and connected to the drain. A toilet that rocks, leaks at the base or runs constantly is usually a flange, wax ring or valve problem, and we repair it in line with these rules.
