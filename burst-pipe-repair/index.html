Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Eustis, FL

Water is spraying, pooling or soaking a ceiling. Shut the main, then call. We stop the damage, repair the pipe and tell you what to document for your insurer.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

A burst pipe can release hundreds of gallons in an hour. The first thing to do is turn off the main water valve, then switch off power to any wet area and call our 24/7 line. We answer at night and on holidays, and we book same-day repairs when available.

Burst pipes in Florida come from age, corrosion, pressure spikes and occasional freezes more often than from a single dramatic event. After the water is off, we find the failed section, repair it and look at the pipe around it, because one failure often signals more. If it points to a pattern, we discuss repiping with you.

What actually breaks pipes in Central Florida homes

Few Eustis burst pipes are freeze damage. The usual causes are these:

  • ✓Corrosion and pinholes in older copper lines.
  • ✓Brittle plastic in older CPVC and polybutylene lines, which crack at fittings.
  • ✓Pressure spikes from a failed regulator or a municipal pressure change.
  • ✓Hose bib failures and fittings damaged by impact or movement.
  • ✓Freeze events, which are rare but real. The Great Freeze of 1894 and 1895 devastated Eustis citrus, and Central Florida can still see hard freezes in some winters.

4

Cracked drain pipes and slow leaks

Not every burst pipe is a pressure line. A cracked drain pipe under a sink or in a wall can send wastewater into cabinets or a ceiling. A pinhole in a copper line sprays a fine mist that stains drywall long before it dumps water on the floor. Both need quick repair.

If you see a stain without an obvious pipe, our leak detection crew can locate the source without opening more drywall than necessary.

5

What a repair visit involves

  1. Shut off and assess. We confirm the main is off, locate the failure and check the nearby pipe.
  2. Price first. You get a clear price covering the repair and any drywall access before we start.
  3. Repair the pipe. We cut out the damaged section, fit new pipe and pressure test.
  4. Restore service. We bring water back on slowly and check all fixtures for leaks.
  5. Paperwork and advice. We write a plain summary of the failure to support your insurance claim and note any other weak spots.
6

Insurance and water damage

Many homeowner policies cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, but not the pipe itself or long-term leaks. Call your insurer quickly, keep photos, receipts and the damaged materials until an adjuster has seen them. Do not discard anything that proves the cause.

Mitigation, meaning removing water and drying, should begin quickly. If drywall and flooring stay wet for days, mold can follow. We can point you to local drying and restoration help, and we work with Mount Dora, Umatilla and Eustis homeowners in the same way.

8

Materials, pressure and code

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, lists the pipe and fitting types approved for water lines, and the IPC limits static pressure to about 80 psi unless a reducing valve is installed. Repairs use approved materials and fittings, and we check pressure after the fix. High pressure is a common hidden reason pipes fail.
