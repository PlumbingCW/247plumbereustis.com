Umatilla calls itself the Gateway to the Ocala National Forest, and its residents include many long-time homeowners. About 35 percent of the town is 65 or older, with a median age of 53.5 at the 2020 census. For those households a clear explanation and a firm price matter as much as speed, and that shapes how we handle calls here.

We dispatch from Eustis, about seven miles south along SR 19, and the line stays open 24/7. The first questions below apply to an emergency, and the rest cover the services Umatilla homeowners ask for most.

2

Septic or sewer: tell us when you call

Umatilla's housing includes properties on city service and some on septic systems or private wells, so the first question the dispatcher asks is which you have. A septic backup can be a full tank or a drain field problem, and a cable cannot fix those. We clear the building drain to the tank and say when a pump-out or a septic specialist is the right next call.

If you are on a private well, there is no public water report to consult, so a lab test is the starting point for any water quality concern. See water treatment for how we approach that.

A town on SR 19

SR 19 runs through the center of Umatilla, about 11 miles south to Tavares and on north through the Ocala National Forest. That corridor is how we reach town from Eustis, and it is also why we serve nearby Altoona from the same dispatch point. The 32702 ZIP code belongs to Altoona, a small community just beyond Umatilla, and we cover it from here.

Incorporated on November 8, 1904, Umatilla has many homes that are decades old, and the plumbing in them often needs attention as it ages. A camera inspection before a repair can save a homeowner from replacing the wrong section.

Plumbing code and permits in Umatilla

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, applies statewide, and each town runs its own permit office. In Umatilla we work to the same International Plumbing Code (IPC) rules that apply in Eustis. Properties with septic follow the Florida Department of Health onsite sewage rules, so tell us which system you have when you call.
