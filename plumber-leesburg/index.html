3

A city with older and newer neighborhoods

Leesburg was settled in 1857 and incorporated in 1875, and it has older neighborhoods near the lakes and downtown alongside newer subdivisions. Older lots may have galvanized or cast iron lines, and newer ones are more likely PEX or PVC. Our approach to a failing line is the same, we look first, then explain the options.

Large households and bigger homes in newer areas often ask about water heater installation and sizing, while older homes ask about repiping and drain repairs. A sewer camera inspection is popular with buyers of older homes.

4

Freezes, lakes and the Leesburg water table

Leesburg's citrus industry was pushed south by freezes in 1983 and 1985, a reminder that Central Florida can see hard cold in some winters. Exposed outdoor lines and hose bibs are the usual victims, and a burst pipe after a cold night is a call we take seriously. Insulating exposed pipes and knowing your main shutoff are cheap protections.

Homes near Lake Harris and Lake Griffin sit close to the water table, and a cracked line may take in groundwater. If your sewer backs up after heavy rain, mention that when you call.

5

Shopping centers, offices and Lake Square Mall

Leesburg has a large commercial base, including Lake Square Mall, which opened in 1980, along with offices and restaurants along US 441 and US 27. Restroom backups and kitchen drains are the common calls for businesses, and our commercial plumbing service handles after-hours work. Tell us whether you own the building or lease, and we will confirm who authorizes the repair.

Plumbing code and permits in Leesburg

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, applies statewide, and each town runs its own permit office. In Leesburg we follow the same International Plumbing Code (IPC) rules that apply in Eustis, including relief valves, traps and backflow protection. We confirm the permit route for your address before work begins.
