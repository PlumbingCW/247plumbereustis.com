Privacy Policy

How we handle your information when you call us or use this website.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

Eustis 24/7 Plumbing respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. We run this website as a call-only service, so there are no forms, accounts or checkout on this site.

Calls to our line

When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address and a description of the plumbing problem so that we can book and perform the work. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you prefer not to be recorded, tell the person who answers at the start of the call.

What we do with your information

We use call details to schedule visits, give you a clear price, perform the work and follow up on it. We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with people who help us run the business, such as scheduling and phone services, and when the law requires it.

Website data

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs, such as your browser type and the pages you visited. Embedded Google Maps on our pages may set cookies or collect data under Google's own privacy policy. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to have it deleted, call us at (321) 463-9969 or write to contact@247plumbereustis.com. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date of the latest version appears below. Last updated November 14, 2026.
