3

Grease traps and interceptors

Restaurants and food service sites rely on a grease trap or interceptor to keep fats from reaching the sewer. A trap that is full does not do its job, and the grease passes through to coat your own lines. We inspect traps, clear the lines they feed and point out where a pump-out is overdue.

Pumping a trap is a scheduled service and the frequency depends on kitchen volume. We recommend a schedule based on what we find and not a generic one, and we keep a note for your next visit.

4

Backflow testing and cross-connection protection

A backflow preventer keeps contaminated water from flowing back into the public supply. Many commercial properties, and some irrigation systems, are required to test their assemblies on a schedule set by the water utility. The Eustis Water Department at (352) 357-5618 can tell you what your account requires.

Backflow testing is a planned service, not an emergency, and it converts best when you call ahead rather than after a notice. We test, record and note any assembly that fails so you can plan repair or replacement.

5

Commercial leak detection, honestly

Many commercial calls are about a leak in a slab, a wall or a buried line serving a unit. We use the same acoustic and thermal methods described on our leak detection page, and in a commercial setting the cost of tracing can be high.

We tell you upfront what we can find and what it will cost, and we are honest when a leak cannot be pinpointed in the budget you have. If another trade is the answer, such as a roofer or HVAC for condensation, we say so.

Older downtown buildings

The Eustis Commercial Historic District holds buildings from the 1880s, including the Iron Block and City Hall. Older commercial buildings may have mixed pipe materials from repairs and additions over the decades, and shutoffs can be hard to find. We map them on the first visit and leave you a list.

If a downtown space needs work in a protected or historic structure, check with the city before altering visible plumbing. The Eustis Building Department at (352) 483-5462 is the starting point. We also serve businesses in Mount Dora and Leesburg.

9

Commercial rules to know

Businesses follow the same Florida Building Code, Plumbing, as homes, with added rules for fixture counts, backflow protection and grease interceptors where food is prepared. The IPC sets these requirements and local inspectors enforce them. We ask about your use, your fixtures and your current inspection status before quoting, so the work meets what your type of business needs.
