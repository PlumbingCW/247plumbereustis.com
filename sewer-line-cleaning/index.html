Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Eustis, FL

A blocked main line backs up every drain below it. We clear it, cut the roots, confirm the flow with a camera and tell you whether the line needs more than cleaning.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

A main line blockage is the clog behind every other clog. When the lowest tub, a floor drain or the toilets begin to back up together, the main is the likely culprit, and the matter is urgent. Our 24/7 line answers at night and on weekends, and while you wait, stop using water and keep drains closed.

Sewer line cleaning clears the line, but it is also diagnostic. What comes out, how far the cable travels and what the camera shows tell us whether this was grease, roots or something that needs a sewer line repair. We say so plainly before you pay.

1

Backing up now: your first steps

Main line backups can spread quickly through the lowest drains in the house.

No water down the drainsHold laundry, dishes and showers until the line is clear.
Cover the floor drainA bubbling floor drain or lowest tub is the first place sewage appears. Keep rugs and cords away.
Locate the cleanoutA capped pipe near the foundation or lawn is the cleanout. Tell us where it is.
Do not chemical-doseOpener chemicals do not clear a main, and they make the work unsafe.
2

Cleanouts, laterals and the main

The cleanout is a capped pipe that gives us direct access to the lateral, the line running from your home to the municipal main or your septic tank. A home without one forces a plumber to remove a toilet or climb to the roof vent, which adds time and cost.

If there is no cleanout, installing one is often the smartest part of a main line visit. It shortens every future service call, and it gives an inspection camera a clean way in.

4

Septic systems outside the sewer

Not every property near Eustis is on city sewer. Some properties outside the city limits, such as in the 32736 ZIP code, and many properties near Umatilla and Howey-in-the-Hills may use septic. A septic backup can come from a full tank, a failed baffle or a saturated drain field, and a cable cannot fix the wrong problem.

If you have a septic system, tell the dispatcher when you call. We clear the building drain line leading to the tank and tell you when a pump-out or a septic specialist is the right next step. See our Umatilla page for how we handle those calls.

5

Storm drains and yard drains

Yard and storm drains that handle roof runoff can clog with leaves, silt and roots. Eustis gets about 51 inches of rain a year, mostly June through September, so a blocked yard drain can flood a patio or garage quickly. We cable or jet these lines the same way we handle sewer laterals.

If a yard drain ties into the sewer, which should not happen, we flag it. Storm water belongs in a separate line, and a mix-up is worth correcting.

6

How a main line cleaning goes

  1. Locate access. We find the cleanout or the best alternative point.
  2. Price first. You hear the price for the cleaning before the cable goes in.
  3. Cable or jet. We clear the blockage, using a cutting head for roots or a jetter for buildup.
  4. Camera confirm. A camera pass shows the line is open and whether damage remains.
  5. Advice. We tell you whether this was a one-time clog or a sign the line needs more.

8

Why cleanouts matter in code and in practice

The International Plumbing Code (IPC) requires cleanouts so that drains can be cleared without damage. We use the cleanout nearest the blockage, run the cable or jet and then confirm flow. If there is no usable cleanout, installing one is a small repair that pays for itself the next time the line clogs.
