How Long Does It Take to Install a Garbage Disposal in Eustis, FL?

A straight swap often takes one to two hours. A first-time install, new wiring or a failing drain can double that.

By the Eustis 24/7 Plumbing team. Published November 14, 2026.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

When the sink already has a garbage disposal and you are replacing it with a similar model, the job usually takes about one to two hours. The old unit comes off its mounting ring, the new one clips on, and the drain and dishwasher connections are reattached. A first-time install in a sink that never had one is a longer job, because the sink flange, the wiring and the drain all need attention.

What slows an install is rarely the disposal itself. It is a corroded drain line under the sink, a power outlet that is missing, a dishwasher drain that needs rerouting or a tight cabinet. Our garbage disposal service team can tell you before the visit whether your sink is a quick swap, and the sections below explain the full picture.

1

JobTypical timeWhat affects it

These are rough ranges, not promises. A plumber can give a better estimate after seeing your sink.

2

The steps of a normal install

  1. Turn off the power and water. Unplug the disposal or turn off its breaker, and close the water supply if work will reach the supply lines.
  2. Disconnect the old unit. Detach the drain and dishwasher hose, then twist the unit off its mounting ring.
  3. Prepare the sink flange. Clean the old putty, set the new sink flange and secure the mounting ring.
  4. Mount the new disposal. Lift it into place, lock it to the ring and connect the drain and dishwasher line.
  5. Connect power and test. Plug it in or wire it per the instructions, run water, check for leaks and run the unit.
3

What can slow the job down

Look at your own sink for these:

  • ✓No outlet under the sink, which means an electrician or a new wire run.
  • ✓A stuck or corroded mounting ring that will not come loose.
  • ✓Old drain pipe that crumbles when disturbed and must be replaced.
  • ✓A dishwasher drain connection with no air gap or high loop.
  • ✓A cabinet so tight that the unit barely fits.

4

Eustis note: permits and local rules

The Eustis Building Department at (352) 483-5462 says plumbing work needs a permit and asks owners to call before starting. A like-for-like disposal swap is often treated as minor, but electrical work for a new outlet may not be. Call before you begin and ask which applies to your job.

If your home is in Leesburg or Umatilla, local offices differ. Our crew knows the process in each town. Because Eustis water is treated with chlorine averaging 1.2 ppm in its 2023 report and the city lists no hardness figure, residue buildup inside a disposal is more likely to come from food and grease than from water chemistry.

5

Choosing a disposal that fits your home

Disposal horsepower is the main choice. A smaller unit handles light use for a couple. A larger one grinds faster and runs more quietly with heavy use, and it costs more. Continuous-feed models start when you flip a switch, and batch-feed models run only when a stopper is in place.

Match the unit to your drain, too. A powerful disposal on a weak drain line just moves clogs further down the pipe. If your kitchen drain already runs slow, have it checked first. A drain that has trouble moving water will not improve when a bigger grinder is added above it.

6

Safety before any install

Turn off the power at the breaker, not just the switch, before you touch the unit. Never put your hand in a disposal, even when it is off, and use tongs or pliers to remove anything stuck. Wear safety glasses when you work under the sink, because old putty and debris can drop.

Check for a leak after the install, and again the next day. A slow drip at the sink flange or the dishwasher inlet is easy to fix when it is fresh, and it can ruin a cabinet floor if it is ignored.

7

Breaking in a new disposal

After the install, follow these habits:

  • ✓Run cold water before, during and after each use.
  • ✓Feed small amounts at a time and avoid fibrous items like celery and corn husks.
  • ✓Skip grease, coffee grounds and bones.
  • ✓Grind a few ice cubes now and then to knock off residue.
  • ✓Never pour a chemical cleaner through the unit.
8

Quick recap before you start

  • ✓A like-for-like swap often takes one to two hours, and a first-time install takes longer.
  • ✓Missing outlets, old drain pipe and tight cabinets cause most delays.
  • ✓Turn off the breaker and never reach into the chamber.
  • ✓Run cold water during every use and keep grease and fibrous food out.
  • ✓If the sink drains slowly before the install, fix the drain first so the new unit has a clear path.
9

What the plumbing code says

A disposal connects to power under the National Electrical Code (NEC) and to the drain through a trap built to the International Plumbing Code (IPC). If there is no receptacle under the sink, an electrician should add one. If the dishwasher drains into the disposal, the hose needs a high loop or an air gap to prevent backflow.
