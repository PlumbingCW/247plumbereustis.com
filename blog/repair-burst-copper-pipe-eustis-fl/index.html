How to Repair a Burst Copper Pipe in Eustis, FL: First Steps and Fixes

Shut off the main, open the taps and call. A temporary clamp can slow a leak, but a permanent copper repair needs the right fittings and tools.

By the Eustis 24/7 Plumbing team. Published November 14, 2026.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

A burst copper pipe can put hundreds of gallons of water into a house within an hour. The first priority is not the repair, it is stopping the flow and protecting people, electronics and the floor. Everything else can wait for a plumber.

Once the water is off, you have time to look at the pipe and decide what to do. Pinhole leaks, split seams and joints that have let go all look different, and each one calls for a different fix. Our burst pipe repair team answers the 24/7 line, and the steps below describe what to do while you wait for us.

1

The first ten minutes

Act in this order:

  1. Shut off the main water valve. It is usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. Open the lowest faucet and a high one. This drains the pipes and relieves pressure.
  3. Turn off the power if water is near outlets. Use the breaker panel and stay out of standing water that touches electrical devices.
  4. Move belongings and start removing water. Lift rugs and furniture, and use towels, buckets or a wet vac.
  5. Call a plumber and take photos. Photograph the pipe and damage for your insurance claim.
2

How copper pipes fail

What you seeLikely causeUsual repair
A pinhole sprayCorrosion from the inside or outside of the pipeCut out the section and replace it, since more pinholes are likely nearby
A split along the pipeFreezing or a pressure spikeReplace the damaged length with new copper or PEX
A joint that has come apartA failed solder or a fitting that has agedRe-sweat the joint or use a push fitting rated for the job
A crushed or dented pipeImpact or ground movementReplace the damaged piece
Several leaks at onceWidespread corrosionPlan a larger repair, see repiping
3

Temporary fixes and why they are only temporary

A pipe repair clamp, a rubber patch with a hose clamp or a push-on coupling can slow or stop a leak while you wait for help. They are useful in an emergency because they buy time. Do not leave them in place for weeks, because the pipe around the damage is usually weak too, and the next failure may be worse.

A permanent repair cuts out the damaged section, cleans and prepares the ends and joins new pipe with soldered fittings, press fittings or push-fit connectors rated for the line. The plumber then pressure-tests the line and checks the nearby pipe for the same weakness. Skipping the test is how patched pipes burst again.

4

Eustis note: heat, rain and what causes pipe failures here

Hard freezes are uncommon in Eustis, though they do happen, and a cold snap can still split an exposed pipe in an attic or on an outer wall. The Great Freeze of 1894 and 1895 is part of local history, and it is a reminder that winters can surprise Central Florida. Corrosion is a more common cause in older homes.

The City of Eustis lists no hardness figure in its 2023 water quality report, and it does not publish a corrosion study, so we do not guess at the cause of pinholes. A plumber can inspect the pipe and tell you what failed. For homes in Mount Dora or Leesburg, call the same 24/7 line. If a repair needs a permit in Eustis, the Building Department at (352) 483-5462 can explain the process.

5

After the repair: dry out and document

Water that stays in walls and floors causes mold and rot. Take these steps:

  • ✓Run fans and a dehumidifier for several days.
  • ✓Cut out wet drywall or insulation if a professional advises it.
  • ✓Keep photos and receipts for your insurance claim.
  • ✓Check the ceiling and walls below the leak for stains over the next weeks.
  • ✓Ask us about a leak detection check if any other fixture seems wrong.
6

Preventing the next burst

Plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve when street pressure is higher than about 80 psi, and a failing regulator can stress every pipe in the house. A cheap gauge on a hose bib shows your pressure. If it reads high, a regulator replacement protects the entire system.

If the burst was one of several leaks in the same house, copper may be wearing out. A plumber can walk the system and tell you whether a repair or a repiping plan makes more sense.

7

Quick recap for the next ten minutes

  • ✓Shut off the main water valve first, then open the lowest tap.
  • ✓Turn off power if water is near outlets or appliances.
  • ✓A clamp or push coupling buys time and is not a permanent fix.
  • ✓Photograph the damage before you clean up so your claim is documented.
  • ✓Call us at any hour. A burst pipe does not wait for business hours.
8

What the plumbing code says

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, lists the pipe materials and fittings approved for water lines, and the International Plumbing Code (IPC) limits static pressure to about 80 psi unless a reducing valve is installed. Permanent repairs use approved parts and are pressure-tested before the pipe is covered.
