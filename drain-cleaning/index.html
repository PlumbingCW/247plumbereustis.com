A drain that stops moving water becomes urgent the moment it backs into a tub, floods a kitchen floor or pushes sewage toward a bathroom. Our 24/7 line takes drain emergencies in Eustis at night, on weekends and on holidays. While you wait, stop running water, close every drain and keep children and pets off wet floors.

Drain cleaning covers three different jobs that look alike from the sink. A slow kitchen line, one clogged toilet and a main line that backs up every fixture are separate problems with separate fixes. We ask which drains work, which do not and where the water appears, because that short description decides whether you need a cable, a camera, hydro jetting or a sewer line repair.

1

When a clogged drain is an emergency

Some clogs can wait until morning and some cannot. Call right away if sewage is rising in a tub or shower, if several fixtures back up together, or if water is spreading toward carpet or electrical outlets. Until the plumber arrives, do this:

Stop the waterDo not flush, run the dishwasher or start the washing machine. Every gallon you add has nowhere to go.
Protect the floorMove rugs and cords away from the backup and lay towels at the edge of the spill.
Skip the chemicalsStore-bought drain openers can burn skin and leave caustic water that our plumber then has to work in.
Note what drainsTell us which fixtures drain normally. A working sink beside a dead tub points to a branch line, not the main.

3

Kitchen sink clogs and what the disposal has to do with them

Kitchen lines clog differently from bathroom lines. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe wall and narrows it a little more with each dishwashing cycle until a rice clump or coffee grounds finish the job. A dishwasher drain hose that ties into the disposal can back up into the dishwasher at the same time.

If the sink stops only when the disposal runs, the jam may be in the disposal and not the pipe. Our plumber checks that first, and a stuck unit is a garbage disposal repair rather than a drain job. Running cold water for a few seconds after every use and scraping plates into the trash keeps this line open longer than any additive.

4

Cables, cameras and jetting: choosing the right tool

A small cable clears hair and soap from tub and sink lines. A larger cable with a cutting head handles roots and heavier buildup in a three or four inch line. When a cable stops short of the cleanout or keeps catching, we run a camera to see why, because that usually means a crack, an offset joint or a section that has sagged.

Jetting sends high-pressure water through the line and scours the pipe wall instead of poking a hole through the blockage. It suits grease, scale and repeat clogs, and we explain it in full on the hydro jetting page. We recommend it only when the camera shows buildup that a cable would leave behind.

5

Bathtub, shower and bathroom sink drains

Bathroom drains rarely fail for mysterious reasons. The usual causes we find are:

  • ✓Hair and soap wound around the stopper or trap arm in a tub or shower.
  • ✓Toothpaste and cosmetic residue that hardens in a bathroom sink trap.
  • ✓Wipes and paper towels flushed down a toilet, which catch at the first bend.
  • ✓A vent problem that lets the line gulp air and drain slowly even when clear.
6

What a drain cleaning visit looks like

  1. Call and describe. The dispatcher records which fixtures are affected and books the next available plumber.
  2. Price before work. The plumber confirms the likely cause and gives you a clear price before touching the line.
  3. Clear the line. Cable, auger or jetter goes in, and the plumber runs water to confirm full flow.
  4. Camera when needed. If the clog returns or the cable catches, a camera shows the pipe condition and we talk through options.
  5. Clean up and advise. We protect floors during the work and tell you what habits keep that line open.

Drain notes for Eustis homes

Eustis gets about 51 inches of rain a year, with June through September the wettest stretch. Saturated ground can push water against older clay or cast iron lines and slow a drain that was fine in spring. If a clog appears only after heavy storms, tell us so we check the lateral and not just the fixture.

Homes inside the city limits generally connect to city sewer, and some properties outside them, such as in the 32736 ZIP code, may use septic systems instead. Tell the dispatcher which you have, because a septic backup needs a different fix from a sewer clog. We serve neighbors in Mount Dora and Leesburg with the same equipment.

9

Traps, vents and the plumbing code

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), requires every fixture trap to hold a water seal and every drain system to be vented. When a drain gurgles, a partly blocked vent can be the cause as well as a clog. We check both, because clearing the pipe does not help if the vent is blocked.
