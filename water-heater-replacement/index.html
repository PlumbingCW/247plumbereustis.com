Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Eustis, FL

When a tank has failed, you need hot water back and the old unit gone. We replace gas and electric water heaters in Eustis and quote the full job before we start.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

A failed tank leaves little time to shop around. If your heater is flooding a garage or closet, shut off the cold supply and the power or gas, then call our 24/7 line. We schedule replacements as same-day appointments when available, even when the failure happens overnight.

Replacement and installation overlap, but they are not the same job. Replacement starts with an emergency, an old tank that will not be repaired, and the plan has to fit the existing space, venting and gas or electric supply. Installation covers upgrades and new locations, which we plan with you in advance.

1

A flooding tank: what to do before we arrive

Replacement calls often start with water on the floor. These steps limit the damage.

Cold valveTurn the shutoff on the cold line above the heater clockwise until it stops.
Energy sourceBreaker off for electric, gas control to off for gas. Never leave a dry-firing element on.
Relief valve dischargeIf the relief line is running hot water, keep clear and wait for the plumber.
Photos for insuranceTake pictures of the tank, the label and the water damage before you clean up.
2

Signs the tank is past repair

A heater tells you it is finished in a few ways:

  • ✓Rust-colored hot water, which points to corrosion inside the shell.
  • ✓Water pooling at the base with no fitting leaking above it.
  • ✓A tank older than about a decade with its first major failure.
  • ✓Repeated pressure relief discharge after valve replacement.
  • ✓A burner or element that fails again within months of a repair.

Choosing a replacement unit

Most Eustis homes replace like for like, and a few move to a larger or higher-efficiency unit.

OptionGood fit whenTrade-off
Standard tank, same sizeYou want the fastest, simplest swapFamiliar and quick, shorter lifespan than tankless
Larger tankThe household has grown or runs out of hot waterNeeds space and sometimes a new pan or stand
Hybrid heat pump tankYou want lower electric use and the room is ventilatedHigher purchase cost, needs clearance and a condensate line

4

How a same-day replacement goes

  1. Confirm the unit. We read your old label for size, fuel and venting, then agree on the replacement model.
  2. Price first. You get a clear price for the unit, parts, haul-away and any code corrections before work starts.
  3. Drain and remove. The old heater is drained, disconnected and carried out.
  4. Set, connect and vent. The new heater is set on a pan where needed, connected with new flex lines and a new relief valve, and vented to code.
  5. Test and walk through. We fill, purge, light or power the unit, check for leaks and show you the shutoffs.
5

Permits, inspection and the City of Eustis

The City of Eustis Building Department says a permit is required for work on plumbing, gas, electrical and mechanical systems, and it asks owners to call before starting if unsure. Its office is at 111 E. Orange Avenue and the phone is (352) 483-5462. The city's page does not address water heaters specifically, so confirm the rule for your project with that office.

We handle the paperwork questions with you during booking, so you are not left holding a tank installed without the right sign-off. Permit rules also differ outside the city, for example in Umatilla or Howey-in-the-Hills, and we check the local rule for your address.

What goes into the price of a replacement

The quote has a few moving parts, and we list each so nothing appears later.

What moves the price
  • $The unit itself: size, fuel type, efficiency and warranty length.
  • $New supply lines, shutoff valves, relief valve and discharge line.
  • $Venting changes for gas units or electrical changes for upgrades.
  • $Drain pan, stand or expansion tank where the setup calls for them.
  • $Haul-away of the old tank and after-hours timing.

Home insurance may cover resulting water damage but rarely covers the heater. Want your real number? Call our 24/7 line.

7

Questions to ask any plumber before a replacement

Ask whether the quote includes the permit, the haul-away, a new relief valve and new flex connectors. Ask who is responsible for repairing drywall if the old tank leaked while it was removed. A good answer is specific. A vague one is a warning.

You can also confirm a plumber's license directly with the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation at myfloridalicense.com. Searching by name or license number takes a minute and is worth doing for any plumber you hire.

8

Code items in a replacement

A replacement is the moment the current code applies. The IPC calls for a pan where a leak could damage the building, a relief valve with a proper discharge pipe and an expansion tank on closed systems. For gas units, the Florida Building Code, Fuel Gas, which is based on the International Fuel Gas Code, governs the gas line and venting. We include these in the price we quote.
