Phase 1B, 138 lots along Pine Meadows Golf Course Road, is listed as approved or under construction, and the 60-lot Grove at Pine Meadows is listed as under review.

This page keeps three things apart: facts the City's public records show, general plumbing guidance, and things we do not know. 2

Newer homes still have plumbing questions

A newer house is not a plumbing-free house. Water heaters, shutoff valves, toilet fill valves, faucet cartridges and irrigation parts wear on their own schedule, and an installation defect can show up at any age. We do not claim a particular failure is common in Pine Meadows. We look at the symptom, test it and explain what we find.

If your home is still under a builder or home warranty, read the terms before you pay for a repair. Some warranties ask you to report a defect to the builder first. A plumber can photograph and document the problem so your claim is clear.

Living near active construction

If you notice a sudden pressure change or discolored water while crews are working nearby, note the time and call your water provider first, because the cause may be on the utility side. If water is coming from a fixture, pipe or heater inside your home, call us.

7

Plumbing permits and code

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is built on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), applies statewide. The City of Eustis Building Department at (352) 483-5462 says plumbing work needs a permit and asks owners to call before beginning. Ask any plumber, including us, who pulls the permit for your job.
