Emergency Leak Detection in Eustis, FL

Most leaks announce themselves as a symptom, a meter that will not stop, a bill that jumped or a stain that grew overnight. We trace the cause before anyone cuts into a wall or a slab.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

Water that is leaking somewhere you cannot see keeps costing money and damaging materials until someone finds it. If a ceiling is dripping, a floor is warm or water is surfacing in the yard, call our 24/7 line now and close the main shutoff at the house or the meter. A fast shutoff limits the damage while we plan the trace.

Leak detection is a diagnosis. We locate the leak first, then explain whether it needs a small repair, a slab leak repair, a burst pipe repair or a line replacement. You choose what happens next, with a clear price before any opening is made.

1

Active leak: what to do before we get there

If water is moving right now, treat it as an emergency.

Main shutoffTurn off the main water valve at the house or the meter box and open one tap to relieve pressure.
Power safetyKeep away from outlets, light fixtures and breaker panels near wet areas.
Catch and photographPlace a bucket under a drip and take photos of every stain for insurance.
Do not cut yetDo not open drywall or flooring before we trace it, because the wet spot is often far from the source.
2

Meter still running or the bill jumped

A running meter with everything off is the clearest sign of a hidden leak, and you can confirm it yourself in a few minutes.

  1. Turn off every fixture. Stop the dishwasher, washing machine and icemaker, and make sure no one is using water.
  2. Read the leak indicator. Most meters have a small triangle or dial that spins when any water flows.
  3. Check the toilets. Add a few drops of food coloring to each tank and wait twenty minutes. Color in the bowl means a silent flapper leak.
  4. Call us if it still spins. A spinning indicator with all fixtures off means a leak in the line or an irrigation system.
  5. Ask your utility. The Eustis Water Department at (352) 357-5618 can answer questions about your meter and readings.

3

Wet ceiling, stained wall or attic leak

Ceiling and wall stains are often not under the leak. Water follows framing, pipes and ductwork, and it can appear a room away from the source. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to follow the wet path back to its origin before any repair is proposed.

Many Central Florida homes run supply lines and an air handler condensate line through the attic. A stain below the attic can be a plumbing leak or a clogged condensate drain, and we sort out which before you pay for the wrong repair.

4

Under the house, in the yard and at the meter box

Buried and hard-to-reach leaks have their own signs.

  • ✓Crawlspace or pier-and-beam leaks show as damp soil, mildew smell or sagging insulation below the floor.
  • ✓Yard leaks appear as a soft green patch, a wet spot in dry weather or the sound of running water near the meter.
  • ✓Meter-box leaks show up as water pooling in the box or a meter that spins with the house valve closed.
  • ✓Service line leaks between the meter and the house often cause low pressure as well as a high bill.
  • ✓Irrigation leaks appear as wet patches, a zone that runs weak or a bill that climbs during watering season.

6

Pools, spas and lake properties

Eustis sits on the shore of Lake Eustis, part of the Harris Chain of Lakes, and many homes have pools or irrigation fed from the same property. A pool that loses more than normal evaporation, or a pump that runs without gaining prime, may be leaking in a supply line. We trace the plumbing side, and pool structure leaks fall to pool specialists.

If the leak is in a line you share with a spa or irrigation zone, pressure testing each branch separates them. We also cover Mount Dora and Howey-in-the-Hills lake homes with the same approach.

7

What leak detection costs

Leak detection pricing is driven by how much needs tracing, not by a flat rate.

What moves the price
  • $The number of lines and areas that must be isolated and tested.
  • $Access to the suspected area, such as slab versus an open attic.
  • $Which tools are required, since tracer gas takes more time than listening.
  • $Whether the work happens at night or on a weekend.
  • $Repair cost once the leak is found, quoted separately and clearly.

Insurance may help with the resulting damage and sometimes with tear-out access, but policies vary. Want your real number? Call our 24/7 line.

8

Pressure testing and code practice

The IPC requires new water piping to be tested under pressure before it is covered, and the same idea guides a leak search. We isolate sections of line, watch a gauge and confirm a leak before anyone cuts into a wall. Plumbing codes also cap normal static pressure at about 80 psi, so we check your regulator at the same visit.
