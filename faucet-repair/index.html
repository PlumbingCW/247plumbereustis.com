Emergency Faucet Repair in Eustis, FL

A dripping tap wastes water, and a valve that will not close can flood a room. We repair faucets, shutoff valves, shower valves and tub fixtures, and we price the job before we start.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

Most faucet problems start small, a slow drip or a handle that feels loose. A faucet that will not shut off, a valve spraying behind a sink or a shower that will not turn off is a real emergency, because the water has nowhere to stop. If you cannot close a fixture valve, shut off the main and call our 24/7 line.

We repair, rebuild or replace faucets depending on what is wrong and what it costs. A new cartridge is far cheaper than a new faucet, and a failed valve behind the wall is a different job from a failed spout. We tell you which situation you have before we begin.

1

3

Shower valves and tub faucets

Bathroom fixtures have their own failure modes.

  • ✓Shower that will not shut off usually means a worn cartridge or valve seat.
  • ✓Temperature swings point to a failed pressure-balancing cartridge.
  • ✓Tub spout diverter that fails sends water to the head when you want the spout.
  • ✓Showerhead leaks at the arm are often a worn washer or a loose connection.
  • ✓Handle that spins freely has a stripped stem or a missing set screw.
4

Supply lines, stop valves and the parts under the sink

A small leak under the sink can ruin a cabinet over time. Braided supply lines wear out, compression fittings loosen and old stop valves corrode. We replace worn supply lines and stop valves as a package, because a shutoff you cannot trust is a hazard.

If a drain beneath the sink is slow or leaking, that is a separate job, and a shower or tub that drains slowly has its own causes. See drain cleaning for the sinks and tubs that clog.

5

7

Hard water, aerators and Eustis faucets

Eustis draws its water from the Floridan Aquifer, and the city's public report does not list a hardness figure. If you see white crust at spouts or clogged aerators, ask the Eustis Water Department at (352) 357-5618 or test your tap. Scale shortens cartridge life and can make a faucet drip sooner than expected.

Our crews also serve Leesburg and Mount Dora homes and carry common cartridges on the truck.

8

Backflow rules for faucets

The International Plumbing Code (IPC) requires hose connections to be protected against backflow, which is why many outdoor faucets carry a vacuum breaker built to ASSE 1011. Indoors, faucets must keep an air gap or a check device so that dirty water cannot be drawn back into the supply. We replace failed parts with ones that keep that protection.
