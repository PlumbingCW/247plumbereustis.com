What to do in a plumbing emergency

The first few minutes decide how much damage you have. Do these while you wait for a plumber.

Shut off the waterTurn the main valve clockwise. It is usually near the meter or where the line enters the house.
Stay clear of electricityIf water is near outlets or appliances, turn off power at the breaker panel and stay out of standing water.
Open the tapsA cold tap at the lowest point drains the pipes and relieves pressure after a burst.
Protect belongingsLift rugs and electronics, soak up water and take photos for your insurance claim.
Smell gas? LeaveDo not use switches or phones inside. Go outside, then call the gas utility or 911.

How an emergency call works

No forms and no waiting for an email. You call and a person answers.

  1. Call and describe the problem. Tell us what you see, hear or smell, and give your address.
  2. We book the next available plumber. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows.
  3. The plumber diagnoses and quotes. You hear the clear price before work starts.
  4. The repair is done and tested. We check for leaks and explain what was fixed and what to watch for.

If a problem is not urgent, such as a dripping faucet, we will book a regular visit and keep the 24/7 line free for people with flooding or backups.
