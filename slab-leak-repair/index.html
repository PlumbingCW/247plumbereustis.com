Emergency Slab Leak Repair in Eustis, FL

Pipes under a concrete slab leak quietly until the floor warms, the bill jumps or water surfaces. We pinpoint the leak and give you honest repair options before anything is broken.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

Most Florida homes are built on a concrete slab, and water lines that run beneath it can leak where no one can see. A slab leak may begin as a warm spot on the floor, a sound of running water at night or a bill that climbs for no reason. If you suspect one, close the main valve and call our 24/7 line, because slow slab leaks can undermine flooring and the soil under it.

The cure is not always breaking the slab. We locate the leak first, then compare a small spot repair with rerouting the line overhead or repiping the affected run. You see each option and its cost before we start, and you decide.

1

Signs of a slab leak

These signs are worth acting on right away:

  • ✓A warm patch on the floor from a hot water line leaking underneath.
  • ✓The sound of running water when everything is off.
  • ✓Water meter spinning with all fixtures closed, see leak detection.
  • ✓Damp carpet, lifting tile or cracked grout in one area.
  • ✓Low water pressure or a hot water tank that runs constantly.
  • ✓Foundation cracks or a musty smell that appears after a period of heavy water use.
2

Suspected slab leak: what to do today

Leaks under the slab can undermine the soil over time, so a quick response matters.

Close the mainIf the meter keeps running, shut the house valve and see if the spin stops.
Check the heaterA hot water slab leak makes the heater cycle. Turn it off if the floor is hot.
Move belongingsLift rugs and furniture off a warm or damp area.

3

How we locate the leak

We isolate each water line and pressure test it to find which one has the leak. Acoustic listening then pinpoints the exact spot, and thermal imaging confirms a hot water line. This avoids broken concrete in the wrong place.

When the line is deep or the signal is weak, tracer gas can help. The goal is a mark on the floor, accurate to a few inches, before any tool touches the slab.

Rerouting often costs less than repeated slab openings if the line keeps failing. We explain why we recommend one over another.

5

The repair, step by step

  1. Mark and protect. We mark the leak and cover flooring and furniture.
  2. Price first. You get a clear price covering the opening, repair and patch.
  3. Open and repair. We cut a small opening, replace the damaged section and pressure test.
  4. Patch. We close the opening with concrete and leave it ready for flooring work.
  5. Follow-up. We check the meter and pressure to confirm the leak is gone.
6

Slabs, soils and older Eustis homes

Central Florida's sandy soils and limestone base shift slowly, and a pipe laid under a slab can be stressed by movement and by the soil's chemistry. Copper lines under older slabs are prone to pinholes where they contact aggressive soil or concrete. Eustis includes many older homes in and around its historic center, and a camera or pressure test tells you the pipe's condition before it fails.

If you are buying an older home, ask about slab plumbing. We handle the same work across Leesburg, Mount Dora and nearby towns.

8

Slab leaks and pipe standards

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, requires water piping under a slab to be of approved material and, where it must be buried, protected. When a slab line fails, we choose between a spot repair, a reroute and a repipe based on the pipe's condition. Any new piping must meet the IPC rules for materials and testing.
