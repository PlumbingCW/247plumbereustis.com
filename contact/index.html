Where we serve

We serve Eustis and nearby Lake County towns by phone. Our plumbers dispatch from Eustis to Mount Dora, Umatilla, Leesburg and Howey-in-the-Hills.

What to tell us when you call

Give the address where the plumbing problem is and describe what you see, hear or smell. Tell us whether you have shut off the water and whether anyone is in danger. Mention the age of the water heater if you know it, and any error codes on a tankless unit.

Before you call in an emergency

Shut off the main water valve if water is flowing. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911. Stay out of standing water near electrical outlets and appliances. Then call and we will book the next available plumber.
