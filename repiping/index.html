Repiping in Eustis, FL

When pipes leak repeatedly, run rusty or lose pressure, repairs stop making sense. We replace failing water lines with new PEX or copper and plan the work to disrupt your home as little as possible.

☎ Call (321) 463-9969

Repiping replaces the supply lines that bring water to every fixture. It is a planned project, but the trigger is often an emergency, the third pinhole leak in a year or a burst section behind a wall. If you have an active leak now, shut the main and call our 24/7 line for a burst pipe repair first, then plan the larger fix.

Not every home needs a full repipe. A partial replacement of one failing branch can be enough, and sometimes a repair is the honest answer. We review the pipe, the leak history and the age of the home, then give you a clear price for each option.

1

Signs your pipes are due for replacement

  • ✓Repeated pinhole leaks in copper pipes, usually in more than one location.
  • ✓Rusty or brown water from galvanized lines corroding from the inside.
  • ✓Low pressure that gets worse over time as scale narrows the pipe.
  • ✓Visible corrosion, green or white crust, at joints and fittings.
  • ✓Gray plastic polybutylene lines, which were widely installed from about the late 1970s to the mid 1990s.
  • ✓Brittle CPVC that cracks near fittings.

3

Partial or whole-house repipe

A partial repipe replaces one failing branch, such as a bathroom group or the line under a slab. It costs less and disrupts less, but leaves old pipe elsewhere. A whole-house repipe replaces everything from the main to each fixture and removes the repeat-failure risk.

If you have had two or more leaks in separate areas, the odds favor a bigger replacement. If a single run has failed and the rest tests sound, a partial job may be the smarter spend. We look at both on site.

4

The repiping process

  1. Inspect and plan. We check the pipe type, access and the likely routing.
  2. Price first. You receive a clear written price and a schedule, including drywall access and repair options.
  3. Protect the home. We cover floors, furniture and set up a plan for water-off periods.
  4. Replace the lines. New supply lines are run, connected, tested and labeled.
  5. Restore and test. We reopen water service, check every fixture, and patch access holes if included.
5

Living in the house during a repipe

Most repipes are done in stages, and the water is off for part of each day. We tell you in advance when and for how long, so you can plan showers and cooking. A typical home takes a few days depending on size and access.

Drywall patching and paint are a separate scope in many quotes. Ask whether texture and paint are included. We are clear about what is and is not in the price.

6

Repiping is priced by the house, not by the foot, and you get a written number.

7

Eustis homes, permits and licensed work

The City of Eustis Building Department at (352) 483-5462 says a permit is required for plumbing work, and it asks owners to call before starting if they are unsure. Repiping is a significant project, so confirm permit requirements for your address before work begins.

You can also verify any plumber's license with the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation at myfloridalicense.com. We serve Mount Dora, Leesburg and the rest of the area under the same standard.

8

Approved pipe and the permit

A repipe is a permitted job. The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, and the IPC behind it list which pipe types are approved for potable water, how pipes must be supported and how the new system must be tested before walls are closed. We plan the job around those rules and handle the inspection.

9

What to do before the repipe crew arrives

A little preparation saves time on the day.

  • ✓Clear access to walls, closets and the area around the water heater.
  • ✓Move furniture and valuables away from the work areas.
  • ✓Tell us about pets, small children and anyone who needs water at set hours.
  • ✓Do not try to shut off water with a stuck valve. Call us if the main will not close.
  • ✓Plan for the water to be off for part of the day.
